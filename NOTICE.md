# Notice

Clipline is a Windows take on [Tendedero](https://github.com/alejandrobujan/tendedero)
by Alejandro Buján, a macOS app that hangs every screenshot on a line at the top
of the screen. The way the line hangs, the measures of the photos, the springs
that swing them, the gestures and the rules for when the line comes down are
ported from Tendedero's Swift source to C# and WPF, with the comments carried
over where they still hold.

Clipline uses neither the Tendedero name nor its icon or artwork, as Tendedero's
license asks of modified versions. Its icon and every picture in this repository
are drawn in code here (`tools/IconMaker`, `src/Clipline/Samples.cs`).
